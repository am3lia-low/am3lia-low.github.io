NLP Research · Central Bank Text

Battle of the BERTsSolo

FinBERT vs RoBERTa-large on classifying FOMC statements as hawkish, dovish, or neutral. Replicates the Trillion Dollar Words finding that the larger general-purpose model wins — then digs into why financial-domain pre-training underperforms on central-bank language: a register mismatch between corporate finance and Fed-speak.

Quant ML · Portfolio Optimisation

Dynamic Crypto Portfolio OptimiserTeam

A full research pipeline asking how temporal features and correlation structure can optimise a dynamic crypto portfolio. Pairs return-forecasting models (XGBoost variants, ARIMA) with covariance forecasters (DCC-GARCH, LSTM) inside a Constrained Mean-Variance Optimiser, rebalancing weekly across 8 coins and benchmarked against 1/N and statistical MV.

§ 02

About

I work where machine learning meets markets and language — building systems that don't just predict, but explain themselves.

Most of my recent work lives at the intersection of quantitative finance and NLP: forecasting portfolio risk, classifying central-bank sentiment, retrieving grounded answers from Fed documents, and optimising portfolios with deep covariance models. I care a lot about the unglamorous parts — hallucination guards, reproducibility, cheap-first pipelines, and benchmarks you can actually trust.

But I also build for the joy of it. A desktop cat that summarises your PDFs. A quiz generator that runs on trending topics. A pipeline that turns books into video essays. The playful projects keep the rigorous ones honest.
